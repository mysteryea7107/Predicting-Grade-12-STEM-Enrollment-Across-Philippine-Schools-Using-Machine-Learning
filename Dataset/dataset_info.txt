DATASET INFORMATION

Source:
Department of Education (DepEd), Philippines

Dataset Source:
https://www.deped.gov.ph/machine-ready-files/

Dataset Description:
This dataset contains school-level information, including school location, school characteristics, grade levels offered, and enrollment counts.

Purpose:
The dataset is used for STAT 516 – Applied Predictive Modeling to demonstrate data selection, preprocessing, exploratory analysis, and predictive modeling.

Notes:
- The raw dataset is kept unchanged.
- Data preprocessing and cleaning are performed separately.
- Variables are documented in the main project notebook.
